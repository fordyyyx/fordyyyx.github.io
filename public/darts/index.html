How it works

The plan reads your darts, not your mood

Every dart you tap in the live scorer becomes data. Each session saves the same headline numbers as before (3-dart average, double rate, Bob's 27, darts per leg) plus the detail behind them: where your T20 misses land, your hit rate on each double, first-9 average, checkout rate in legs, and how consistent your visits are.

  • Two levels. Your rolling average (last 7 sessions) sets your scoring level; your rolling double rate sets your doubles level. Every drill target follows one of them.
  • Weaker side gets more time. If one level is lower, five minutes move to it that day.
  • Your weak doubles come up more. Once a common double has 15+ darts logged, Match doubles swaps in your two weakest, and One-dart doubles picks weak ones more often.
  • Dip. Last 5 sessions more than 8% below the 10 before, double rate down more than 15%, or three sessions rated 1–2 in a row: every day becomes a Reset session one level easier, until the numbers come back.
  • Plateau. After 21 sessions, if the last week is within 2% of three weeks ago, scoring moves to the 19s, doubles go one-dart, and targets step up.
  • Retest every four weeks.

Entering darts quickly

  • Scoring pad is laid out like the board around your target: left neighbour on the left, right neighbour on the right. Tap where each dart landed. "Other" opens the full board.
  • Doubles pad: Hit; Single = landed in the number's single bed (missed inside); Outside = over the wire, no score; Wide = the neighbouring number.
  • Full pad (checkouts and legs): pick Double or Treble, then the number. It drops back to single after each dart. Busts are handled for you.
  • Pin board (Bull drill and all doubles drills): tap where the dart landed on the zoomed board. The score works itself out, and the miss is saved in millimetres so Insights can show your grouping and which way you miss. Drag to fine-tune; press near your last pin to move it. "Use buttons" switches a block back to the old pad.
  • Undo steps back one dart. Close the runner mid-session and it resumes where you left it.
